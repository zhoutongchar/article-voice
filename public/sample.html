A little room for wonder

The first thing you notice is the quiet. Not silence, exactly, but the soft sound of a city waking up. A bicycle rolls past the window. Somewhere downstairs, a kettle begins to sing.

A café beside a quiet street has warm windows, a small reading corner, and a place to sit. Morning light moves slowly across the table, and for a little while there is nothing to hurry.

We spend so much of our time looking for the next thing that we forget to notice the thing in front of us. A walk through the neighborhood can become an entirely different experience when we leave our headphones at home.

There is a garden at the end of the street. In spring, its gate almost disappears behind the climbing roses. In autumn, leaves gather on the path and make a sound like paper when you walk across them.

You do not need to go far to find something worth noticing. Sometimes all it takes is a familiar voice, a comfortable pace, and a little room for wonder.
